Questions, answered

Does All5Starz filter out bad reviews?
No. Every customer who opens your link gets your Google review button, whatever rating they'd give. Private feedback is an extra option beside it, never a gate. Google doesn't allow businesses to ask only happy customers for reviews, and All5Starz is built to follow that rule.
How long does setup take?
About a minute. Pick your business, enter the email where private feedback should go, and you'll get your link and QR code.
How do I share my link?
Any way you like: text it after a visit, add it to your emails, set the counter card by the register, or print it on the back of your business card. Templates are included.
Do I need a Google Business Profile?
Yes. Your link sends customers to your Google review page, so you need a Google Business Profile first.
What happens to private feedback?
It goes straight to your email so you can follow up. The customer can still leave a Google review too.
What if I want to cancel?
Cancel anytime. Cancel during the 30-day trial and you won't be charged.
